LEARN QR SAFETY

Safety guide

Simple checks to remember when scanning QR codes in shops, colleges, emails and public places.

01

Check the real domain

Read the address carefully. Small spelling changes or look-alike characters can point to a different website.

02

Do not hurry after scanning

Fake notices often create urgency. Stop and verify the sender before entering personal information.

03

Be careful with payments

Check the payee name and amount inside your payment application. Never share your UPI PIN.

04

HTTPS is not a guarantee

Encryption protects data in transit, but a scam website can also use HTTPS.

05

Avoid replaced QR stickers

In public places, check whether a new sticker has been pasted over the original code.
